# Rad TV - AI-Powered Video Platform for Creators and Consumers

> Rad TV is a two-sided subscription video platform connecting content creators with consumers. AI-powered content enhancement tools for creators, private streaming and sideloading for consumers. Available on web, iOS, Android, Android TV, Apple TV, PS4, PS4 VR (PSVR1), PS5, and PS5 VR (PSVR2).

## Quick Overview

Rad TV serves two primary audiences:
1. **Content Creators**: Professional AI tools for video enhancement, viral prediction with actionable recommendations, and multi-platform monetization
2. **Consumers**: Stream the Rad TV library, or use private video cloud storage with sideloading to PS5/PSVR2, DLNA, uPnP, RSS feeds, and Plex/Jellyfin compatibility

## For Content Creators - AI Enhancement Tools

### AI Video Upscaling
Automatically enhance video quality using machine learning models:
- Upscale 1080p content to 4K or 8K resolution
- Uses Real-ESRGAN and ESPCN neural networks trained on millions of videos
- SwinIR integration planned for even higher-quality results
- Batch processing for entire content libraries
- Preserves original files while creating enhanced versions
- No need to re-record or re-shoot content
- Typical use case: YouTubers improving quality of older videos to match current standards

### Viral Thumbnail & Title Prediction with Recommendations
AI-powered analytics that predict content performance before publishing:
- Computer vision analysis of thumbnail images for clickthrough potential
- Natural language processing of video titles for engagement scoring
- Predictive scoring from 0-100 for viral potential
- **Actionable recommendations**: overall assessment, strengths analysis, and specific improvements to make
- A/B testing recommendations for thumbnails and titles
- Training data from millions of successful viral videos across platforms
- Helps creators optimize for maximum views before going live
- Typical use case: TikTok and YouTube creators testing which thumbnail will perform best and getting AI feedback on how to improve it

### Multilingual AI Subtitling
Automatic subtitle generation and translation:
- Speech-to-text in original language using transformer models
- Automatic translation to 40+ languages including Spanish, Hindi, Mandarin, Arabic, Portuguese, French, German, Japanese, Korean
- Accurate timing and synchronization
- Downloadable SRT/VTT files for use on other platforms
- Typical use case: Educational content creators reaching global audiences, international filmmakers, podcasters expanding reach

### Audio Upmixing
AI-powered audio enhancement:
- Convert stereo audio to immersive 5.1 and 7.1 surround sound
- Neural network-based audio separation and spatial positioning
- Professional-quality sound without expensive equipment
- Enhances viewer experience for gaming content, films, and music videos
- Typical use case: Independent filmmakers and gaming streamers improving production value

### Creator Monetization Features
- Direct fan subscriptions and pay-per-view content
- Multi-platform content delivery (web, iOS, Android, Android TV, Apple TV, PS4, PS5, PSVR1, PSVR2)
- Built-in payment processing and creator payouts
- Revenue sharing that favors creators over traditional platforms

## Technical Capabilities

### AI Models and Technology
- **Video Upscaling**: Real-ESRGAN, ESPCN super-resolution models (SwinIR planned)
- **Audio Processing**: Neural network-based upmixing to 5.1/7.1 surround
- **Subtitle Generation**: Transformer-based speech recognition and translation (40+ languages)
- **Viral Prediction**: Computer vision for thumbnails, NLP for titles, with actionable recommendation engine
- **Content Delivery**: Multi-CDN infrastructure for global streaming
- **GPU Processing**: Tesla T4 GPU instances for video processing with CUDA acceleration

### Video Format Support
Input formats: MP4, AVI, MOV, MKV, WEBM, FLV, MPEG, WMV, 3GP, and all standard video codecs
Output: Optimized for each platform with automatic transcoding
